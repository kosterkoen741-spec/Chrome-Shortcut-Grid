# Chrome Shortcut Grid

**Your new tab, your rules.**

[![Chrome Web Store](https://img.shields.io/badge/Chrome_Web_Store-v1.11-blue.svg)](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/custom-shortcut-grid/mjjpaloegbnjnkemhajhgnimblaffgoo)
[![Languages](https://img.shields.io/badge/Languages-12_Supported-brightgreen.svg)](#supported-languages)

Transform the way you browse with **Chrome Shortcut Grid**. Tired of Chrome's default, limited "New Tab" page? This extension gives you full control back over your homepage with a completely customizable grid for all your shortcuts—supporting up to 8 rows!

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- **Fully Customizable Grid:** Choose exactly which websites you want at your fingertips. Effortlessly add your favorite tools, news portals, or work dashboards with up to 8 customizable rows.
- **Minimalist Frosted Glass Design:** Enjoy an elegant, semi-transparent frosted glass design that creates a clean, calm aesthetic. It blends seamlessly with your chosen background.
- **Productivity-Focused:** Your essential shortcuts are always just one click away. Stop wasting time searching through endless menus or cluttered bookmark bars.
- **Integrated Search Bar:** Experience the familiar, reliable Google search right from your new tab, completely distraction-free.
- **User-Friendly Interface:** Easily add, edit, or organize new shortcuts on the fly via the intuitive pop-up menu.
- **Lightweight & Fast:** Engineered specifically for speed and performance, ensuring your browser never slows down while optimizing your workflow.

---

## 💡 Why Install Chrome Shortcut Grid?

In a world where we constantly juggle dozens of open tabs, **Chrome Shortcut Grid** brings focus, structure, and order back to your browser.

- **Instant Access:** Boost your daily efficiency by organizing your most visited websites neatly in a structured grid.
- **Visual Peace:** The elegant semi-transparent UI looks sleek and professional while providing a calm visual environment for your daily tasks.
- **Tailored for Everyone:** Whether you are a developer, student, creator, or professional, transform your empty tab into a powerful, personalized portal to the web.

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. **Install:** Download the extension directly from the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/custom-shortcut-grid/mjjpaloegbnjnkemhajhgnimblaffgoo).
2. **Open a New Tab:** Click on `+` to open a fresh tab in Chrome.
3. **Customize Your Grid:** Click the extension popup icon to add, edit, or adjust your row count (up to 8 rows) and shortcuts.
